## Where the argument stands (settled)
Validated on n = 35 with proper controls (see `reports/audit_n35/`):
- A linear probe on EEGPT's frozen embeddings decodes imagery vs rest ~71% within subject, on par
  with feature-engineered baselines.
- The representation is **dominated by aggregate, largely aperiodic power** (Spearman r ≈ 0.24–0.32).
- A **small, consistent trend** toward a sensorimotor mu component survives parameterized, spatially
  controlled analysis (mu ≈ 0.04 above non-motor control bands, p ≈ 0.001; central-weighted in
  channel space, not source localization; above a permutation null); beta shows no reliable trend.
  Framed as a trend, not an established component.
- Methodological point: the evidence for rhythm specificity depends on the analysis, since raw band
  power conflates periodic/aperiodic activity and spatial averaging inflates apparent specificity.
