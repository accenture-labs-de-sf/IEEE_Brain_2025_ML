## Where the argument stands (settled analysis)
Validated on n = 35 with proper controls (see `reports/audit_n35/`):
- EEGPT decodes imagery vs rest ~71% within subject, on par with feature-engineered baselines.
- Its representation is **dominated by aggregate, largely aperiodic power** (~0.24 to 0.32).
- A **small, consistent trend** toward a sensorimotor mu component survives parameterized,
  spatially controlled analysis (mu ~0.04 above non-motor control bands, central-weighted, above a
  permutation null); beta shows no reliable trend. Framed as a trend, not an established component.
- Methodological point: whether the model looks rhythm-specific depends on the analysis, raw band
  power conflates periodic/aperiodic activity and spatial averaging inflates apparent specificity.
